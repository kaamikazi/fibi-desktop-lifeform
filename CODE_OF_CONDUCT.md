# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Fibi Companion welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience
levels. Be respectful, patient, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent
while taking impact seriously.

Positive behavior includes:

- giving actionable technical feedback without personal attacks
- respecting privacy, consent, accessibility, and user safety
- crediting ideas, code, art, and research appropriately
- helping newcomers understand project conventions
- accepting correction and resolving disagreements with evidence

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
conduct, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate secret exposure,
malicious code, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project
chat, events, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
owner's published GitHub contact channel. Include only information needed to
understand the incident. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect privacy
where possible, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove
participants based on severity and pattern.

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and should ask another trusted
maintainer to review a report when impartial handling is not possible.
