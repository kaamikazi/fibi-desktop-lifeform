# Fibi GUI Verification Checklist

Use this after installing PyQt6 locally.

- Launch with `python app.py`.
- Send `hi` and confirm an instant local reply.
- Drag Fibi and confirm position persists after restart.
- Click or pet Fibi and confirm a tiny reaction.
- Tray: hide, show, nap, wake, open Notebook, open Settings, quit.
- Settings: toggle TTS, sounds, idle bubbles, memory learning, and save.
- First-run setup: reopen from Settings and finish/skip.
- Notebook: open, view memories, export local bundle.
- Nap/wake: use tray or `/nap` and `/wake`.
- Quiet mode: `/quiet`, `/quiet off`.
- Model status: `/model status`.
- Performance: `/perf`, `/perf reset`.
- Health checks: `/health`, `/assets`.
- Autostart: `/autostart on confirm`, check Startup folder, then `/autostart off`.
- Shutdown/relaunch: confirm Fibi restores position and does not show scary crash text after clean quit.
